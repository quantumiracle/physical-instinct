Robot Tech Routes机器人技术路线

Four routes to getting robots to do useful work, compared on the same terms: what the model outputs, where its data comes from, how far real-robot evidence goes, and what still blocks deployment. Each route has a page with a timeline, and in-depth notes sit under the route they belong to.让机器人干活的四条主流路线，用同一组维度对比：模型输出什么、数据从哪来、真机证据到哪一步、离落地还差什么。每条路线有自己的页面和时间线，深度笔记挂在所属路线下面。

Route 01

VLM brain & agentsVLM 大脑 / Agent

e.g. Astra-style assistants, Claude-driven agents例：Astra 类助手、Claude 驱动的 Agent

A general vision-language model understands the scene and the instruction, plans, and calls skills or writes code. It does not emit motor commands itself.通用视觉语言模型负责理解场景和指令、做规划，再调用技能或写代码；它本身不直接输出电机指令。

Route 02

VLA & world-action modelsVLA / WAM

Vision-language-action models; world-action models that predict the future and the action together视觉-语言-动作模型；同时预测未来画面和动作的世界-动作模型

One model maps pixels and language straight to actions, trained on large teleoperation datasets on top of web-scale pretraining. WAMs add video or world prediction to the same model.一个模型从图像和语言直接输出动作，在大规模遥操作数据和互联网预训练的基础上训练；WAM 在同一个模型里再加上视频或世界预测。

Route 03

Sim2Real

RL in GPU-parallel simulation, zero-shot transfer to the real robot在 GPU 并行仿真里做强化学习，零样本迁移到真机

The RL algorithm has barely changed since 2023. Progress came from engineering that aligns sim with reality: contact simulation, system identification, reset distributions and teacher→RGB-student distillation.2023 年以来 RL 算法本身几乎没变，进展来自让仿真对齐现实的工程：接触仿真、系统辨识、reset 分布、教师→RGB 学生蒸馏。

Route 04

Skill policies技能策略

Grasping, pick-and-place and other single skills抓取、pick-place 等单项技能

A specialist model per skill, tuned for reliability rather than breadth, usually wrapped in classical planning. Defined by method and goal, not by task: a VLA that also picks things up belongs to Route 02.每个技能一个专用模型，追求可靠性而不是广度，通常外面套经典规划。按方法和定位划分而不是按任务：同样会抓取的 VLA 归第 02 条。
